# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

## Annual PTO Allocation
- Full-time employees: 20 days per calendar year
- Part-time employees (20+ hours/week): 10 days per calendar year
- Part-time employees (<20 hours/week): 5 days per calendar year

## Types of PTO
1. **Vacation**: Discretionary time off for personal use, rest, and recreation
2. **Sick Leave**: Time off for employee or family member illness (requires documentation for absences >3 days)
3. **Personal Days**: 2 personal days per year for urgent personal matters

## Requesting PTO
- Submit requests through the PTO management system at least 2 weeks in advance (or as soon as possible for emergencies)
- Manager must approve requests within 3 business days
- During peak periods, requests must be submitted earlier to ensure adequate coverage

## Carryover and Payout
- Unused PTO does not carry over to the next calendar year
- PTO is not paid out upon termination except as required by local law
- Employees should take regular PTO to maintain work-life balance

## Blackout Periods
- No more than 50% of the team can be out during critical project milestones
- Year-end holidays (December 20 - January 2) require manager approval

## Sabbatical
After 5 years of service, employees may request an extended sabbatical of 1-3 months for professional development or personal renewal.
