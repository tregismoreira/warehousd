# Compliance and Legal Obligations

## Overview
This document outlines key compliance requirements that all employees must follow. Failure to comply may result in disciplinary action, termination, or legal consequences.

## Anti-Discrimination and Harassment
- The company maintains a zero-tolerance policy for discrimination and harassment based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or sexual orientation
- All complaints must be reported to HR and will be investigated promptly
- Retaliation against complainants is strictly prohibited

## Conflicts of Interest
- Disclose any personal or financial relationships that could create a conflict of interest
- Employees cannot work for competitors or maintain business interests that conflict with company operations
- Gifts and entertainment valued over $50 USD must be reported to compliance

## Data Privacy and GDPR Compliance
- Personal data of EU residents must be handled in compliance with GDPR regulations
- Data processors must be authorized and under contract
- Individuals have rights to access, correct, and delete their data upon request
- Data breach notifications must be made within 72 hours to applicable authorities

## Regulatory Compliance
- Financial reporting must comply with SOX and accounting standards
- Export control regulations apply to technology and services
- Healthcare data (if applicable) must comply with HIPAA
- Industry-specific certifications and compliance requirements vary by team

## Whistleblower Protection
Anonymous reporting is available through the ethics hotline. Whistleblower protections apply to all good-faith reports of policy violations or illegal activities.
