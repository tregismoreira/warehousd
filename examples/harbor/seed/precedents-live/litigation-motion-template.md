# Precedent — Motion Template (General Federal Practice)

This template provides the standard structure our litigation group uses for dispositive and
discovery motions in federal court: a statement of relief requested, a factual background section,
an argument section organized by legal standard, and a proposed order.

Attorneys should confirm the applicable local rules for page limits and briefing schedules before
filing, since these vary by district.
