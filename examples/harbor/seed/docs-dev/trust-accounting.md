# Trust Accounting (IOLTA) Policy

## Overview
Every attorney who holds client funds must deposit them into the firm's IOLTA trust account,
never into an operating account. Commingling client funds with firm funds is a serious ethics
violation and grounds for disciplinary action, so this policy is treated as non-negotiable.

## Handling Client Funds
- Retainers and settlement proceeds are deposited into the trust account immediately upon receipt.
- Funds may only be disbursed once they are earned (for retainers) or once the underlying
  transaction has cleared (for settlements).
- Each client's funds are tracked in a separate ledger within the trust account; the firm never
  relies on the bank balance alone to determine a given client's available funds.

## Reconciliation
The trust account is reconciled monthly against both the bank statement and the individual client
ledgers. Any discrepancy, however small, is investigated and resolved before the next billing
cycle — an unreconciled trust account is one of the most common triggers for a bar audit.

## Reporting
Trust account balances and disbursement history are available to the responsible attorney on
request, and the finance team provides a summary report to each client upon closing of their
matter.
