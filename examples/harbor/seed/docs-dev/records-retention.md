# Records Retention Policy

## Overview
This policy governs how long the firm retains client files, billing records, and internal
correspondence, and the process for secure destruction once a retention period lapses. Consistent
retention practice is both a malpractice-insurance requirement and a professional-responsibility
obligation under the applicable rules of professional conduct.

## Retention Periods
- **Closed client matters**: retained for a minimum of 7 years after final billing, longer for
  minors, estates, or matters with a statute of limitations that runs past the standard window.
- **Trust accounting records**: retained for 7 years per IOLTA compliance requirements, regardless
  of matter status.
- **Conflict-check records**: retained indefinitely, since a firm may need to screen against a
  closed matter decades later.
- **Internal correspondence and drafts not part of the client file**: retained for 3 years, then
  eligible for destruction.

## Destruction Process
Files eligible for destruction are flagged by the records team, reviewed by the responsible
attorney for any litigation hold, and destroyed via a certified shredding vendor. Electronic
records are permanently deleted, not merely archived, once a hold-free file clears its retention
window.

## Litigation Holds
Any pending or reasonably anticipated litigation suspends the normal retention schedule for the
affected files until the hold is formally released by the general counsel's office.
