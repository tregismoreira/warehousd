# Engagement Letter

Harbor Law LLP is pleased to represent Beacon Manufacturing in connection with the review and
renegotiation of its principal supply agreements. This letter confirms the scope of our
representation and the terms on which our services will be billed.

Our engagement covers review of the existing supply contracts, negotiation of amended terms with
the client's key suppliers, and advice on termination rights under the current agreements. Fees
are billed monthly at the attorneys' standard hourly rates against a retainer held in trust.
