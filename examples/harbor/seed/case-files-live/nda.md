# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

Ridgeline Property Group and a prospective joint-venture partner wish to explore a potential
development project and, in connection with that exploration, may disclose confidential business
and financial information to one another. This agreement governs the handling of that information.

Each receiving party agrees to use the disclosing party's confidential information solely to
evaluate the potential joint venture and to protect it with the same degree of care it uses for
its own confidential information. This agreement remains in effect for two years from the date of
last disclosure.
