# Response to Requests for Production

Defendant responds to plaintiff's First Set of Requests for Production as follows. Defendant
objects to Requests Nos. 4 through 11 to the extent they seek documents protected by the
attorney-client privilege or work-product doctrine, and to the extent they are not proportional to
the needs of the case.

Subject to and without waiving these objections, defendant will produce non-privileged documents
responsive to the requests within its possession, custody, or control that can be located after a
reasonable search. Defendant reserves the right to supplement these responses as discovery
proceeds and additional responsive material is identified.

Defendant denies that its shipment records are incomplete and will produce the warehouse system
export referenced in plaintiff's requests, subject to the entry of an appropriate protective order
governing confidential business information.
