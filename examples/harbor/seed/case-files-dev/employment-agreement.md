# Executive Employment Agreement

This Employment Agreement sets forth the terms under which the executive will serve as Chief
Operating Officer of the client company. The executive's initial term runs three years, renewing
automatically for successive one-year terms unless either party gives ninety days' notice of
non-renewal.

Compensation includes a base salary reviewed annually by the board, an annual performance bonus
target, and an equity grant vesting over four years. The agreement includes standard
confidentiality, non-solicitation, and twelve-month non-compete provisions enforceable to the
extent permitted by applicable state law.

Upon termination without cause, the executive is entitled to severance equal to twelve months'
base salary and continued health coverage, contingent on execution of a release of claims in favor
of the company.
