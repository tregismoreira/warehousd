# Deposition Summary — Defendant's Operations Manager

This memorandum summarizes the deposition of the defendant's operations manager, taken April 18,
2025. The witness testified for approximately six hours and confirmed that the shipment logs at
issue were generated automatically by the defendant's warehouse system and retained for at least
three years under the defendant's own document policy.

The witness was unable to explain several gaps in the logs corresponding to the dates in question,
and testified that a system migration in late 2024 may have affected record completeness. This
testimony materially undercuts defendant's earlier written discovery response asserting the logs
were complete and unaltered.

Key exhibits marked during the deposition included the warehouse system export, the document
retention policy, and an internal email describing the system migration. Follow-up written
discovery regarding the migration is recommended before the close of fact discovery.
