# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

The parties wish to explore a potential business relationship and, in connection with that
exploration, may disclose confidential business, technical, and financial information to one
another. This agreement governs the handling of that information.

Each receiving party agrees to use the disclosing party's confidential information solely to
evaluate the potential relationship, to protect it with the same degree of care it uses for its
own confidential information, and to indemnify the disclosing party for any loss caused by an
unauthorized disclosure by its employees or agents.

This agreement remains in effect for three years from the date of last disclosure. Confidential
information does not include information that is or becomes publicly available through no fault of
the receiving party, or that was already known to the receiving party before disclosure.
