# Motion to Compel Discovery

Plaintiff moves this Court for an order compelling defendant to produce documents responsive to
Requests for Production Nos. 4 through 11, served on January 6, 2025. Defendant's responses,
served February 5, 2025, asserted boilerplate objections without a privilege log and withheld
responsive electronic communications without justification.

Defendant has not met its burden of showing that the withheld material is privileged or otherwise
protected from discovery. Counsel conferred by telephone on March 3, 2025 in a good-faith attempt
to resolve this dispute without court intervention, but the parties remain at an impasse over the
scope of the withheld production.

Plaintiff respectfully requests that the Court order defendant to produce all responsive documents
within fourteen days, along with a privilege log identifying any document withheld on privilege
grounds, and award plaintiff its reasonable expenses in bringing this motion.
