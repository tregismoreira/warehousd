# Conflict Waiver Letter

Our conflicts check identified that Harbor Law LLP currently represents another client in an
unrelated commercial lease matter with a party affiliated with your prospective counterparty in
this transaction. We do not believe this creates a material conflict, but we are required to
disclose it and obtain your informed consent before proceeding.

The two matters are factually and legally unrelated: no confidential information from the other
representation bears on this transaction, and no attorney working on your matter has access to the
other client's confidential files. Ethical screens are in place as an additional precaution.

By signing below, you consent to our continued representation in this matter notwithstanding the
disclosed relationship, and acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to consult independent
counsel regarding this waiver.
