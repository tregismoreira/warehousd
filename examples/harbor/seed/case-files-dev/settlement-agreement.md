# Settlement Agreement and Release

This Settlement Agreement resolves all claims asserted in the above-captioned matter. In
consideration of the payment described below, the parties release each other from any and all
claims, known or unknown, arising out of or related to the dispute, except for claims arising from
a breach of this agreement itself.

Each party agrees to indemnify and hold the other harmless from any third-party claim arising from
its own breach of the representations made in this agreement. Neither party admits liability, and
this agreement may not be construed as an admission of wrongdoing by either side.

The terms of this settlement, including the payment amount, are confidential and may not be
disclosed except as required by law, court order, or to each party's professional advisors bound
by an equivalent duty of confidentiality.
