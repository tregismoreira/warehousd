# Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement

As a condition of employment, the executive assigns to the company all right, title, and interest
in any invention, work of authorship, or trade secret conceived or developed during the term of
employment that relates to the company's business or results from work performed for the company.

This assignment does not extend to inventions developed entirely on the executive's own time,
without use of company resources, and unrelated to the company's actual or anticipated business —
consistent with the carve-out required under applicable state invention-assignment statutes.

The executive agrees to execute any documents reasonably necessary to perfect the company's
ownership of assigned intellectual property, including patent applications, both during and after
employment, at the company's expense.
