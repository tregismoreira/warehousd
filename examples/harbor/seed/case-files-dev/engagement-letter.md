# Engagement Letter

Harbor Law LLP is pleased to represent the client in connection with the acquisition and
leaseback of the client's distribution facility. This letter confirms the scope of our
representation, the attorneys assigned to the matter, and the terms on which our services will be
billed.

Our engagement covers due diligence on title and zoning, negotiation of the purchase and
leaseback agreements, and coordination with the client's lender through closing. Work outside this
scope, including any subsequent litigation arising from the transaction, will require a separate
engagement.

Fees are billed monthly at the attorneys' standard hourly rates, with a retainer held in our trust
account against final invoices. The client may terminate this engagement at any time upon written
notice, subject to payment for services already rendered.
