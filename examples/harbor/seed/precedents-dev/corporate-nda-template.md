# Precedent — Mutual NDA Template (Delaware)

This template is the firm's standard mutual non-disclosure agreement for early-stage commercial
discussions, drafted under Delaware law. It uses a three-year confidentiality term by default and
includes standard carve-outs for publicly available information and independently developed
material.

For transactions involving trade secrets rather than ordinary business information, attorneys
should extend the confidentiality term and add an explicit trade-secret preservation clause rather
than relying on the default term, since a fixed expiration can inadvertently terminate trade-secret
protection.

Deal teams should confirm the counterparty's preferred governing law before sending this template,
as several repeat counterparties require New York law instead of Delaware.
