# Precedent — Executive Offer Letter Template (California)

This template covers the standard terms for a senior-level offer letter under California law,
including at-will employment language, base compensation, bonus eligibility, and equity grant
terms. It intentionally omits a non-compete clause, since California law voids most non-compete
provisions regardless of how they are drafted.

Where the client wants post-employment restrictions, use the narrower non-solicitation and
confidentiality clauses included here instead, and flag for the client that broader restrictions
will likely be unenforceable in this jurisdiction.

Severance terms should be confirmed with the client's HR team before each use, since standard
severance multiples vary by seniority band and have changed twice in the past year.
