# Precedent — Motion Template (General Federal Practice)

This template provides the standard structure our litigation group uses for dispositive and
discovery motions in federal court: a statement of relief requested, a factual background section
drawing exclusively from the record, an argument section organized by legal standard, and a
proposed order.

Attorneys should confirm the applicable local rules for page limits, meet-and-confer certification
requirements, and briefing schedules before filing, since these vary significantly by district and
this template does not substitute for that review.

Prior successful motions using this structure are indexed by matter number in the firm's precedent
database; consult the responsible attorney on a comparable matter before departing materially from
this format.
