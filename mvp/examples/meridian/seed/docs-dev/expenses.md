# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Eligible Expenses
The following business-related expenses may be reimbursed:
- **Travel**: Airfare, ground transportation, lodging at reasonable rates
- **Meals**: Up to $75 USD per day (with receipts)
- **Conference Registration**: Pre-approved professional development events
- **Client Meetings**: Meals and entertainment directly related to client business (up to $150 USD per event)

## Travel Guidelines
- Book flights and hotels in advance for better rates
- Economy class required for flights under 6 hours
- Business class requires VP-level approval for longer flights
- Rental cars: economy or mid-size only (upgrade requires justification)

## Submission Process
1. Collect itemized receipts for all expenses
2. Submit expense report within 30 days of incurring the expense
3. Include business purpose and attendees for all expenses
4. Reports must be approved by the employee's manager

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses
- Personal items (clothing, toiletries, entertainment not related to business)
- Alcoholic beverages beyond meal amounts
- Traffic violations, parking tickets, or other legal penalties
- Personal travel insurance

## Per Diem
For trips exceeding 3 days, employees may request a per diem allowance instead of detailed expense reporting. Current rates: $60 USD per day (CONUS).
