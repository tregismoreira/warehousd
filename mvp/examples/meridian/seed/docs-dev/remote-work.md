# Remote Work Policy

## Overview
This policy outlines guidelines for employees working remotely. Remote work arrangements are approved on a case-by-case basis and must align with business needs and team collaboration requirements.

## Eligibility
- Employees in roles that can be effectively performed remotely (e.g., software engineers, designers, writers)
- Minimum 6 months tenure with the company
- Prior performance reviews demonstrating reliability and self-direction

## Work Schedule
- Core hours: 10 AM - 3 PM in the employee's timezone
- Flexibility outside core hours for meetings across timezones
- Full-time employees must work a minimum of 40 hours per week
- Lunch breaks and time off must be tracked through the standard system

## Equipment and Technology
- Company provides laptop and necessary equipment
- Employee responsible for maintaining secure internet connection (minimum 25 Mbps download)
- All work must be conducted on company-provided devices or approved personal devices with MDM enrollment
- Two-factor authentication required for all company systems

## Performance Expectations
Remote employees are held to the same performance standards as in-office staff. Regular check-ins with managers ensure alignment on goals and progress.
